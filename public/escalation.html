          n8n Production Pipeline

          Supplier Escalation & Risk Monitoring Engine

          End-to-end architecture: Ingestion → 6-Criteria Scoring → AI Briefing → Human Approval → Email Dispatch

          Stage 1: Ingestion
          Daily Trigger & Sheets

          Fires daily at 08:00 AM (or manual webhook) and reads Google Sheet PO_Tracker across 19 operational columns.

          Nodes: ScheduleTrigger, Read PO Tracker
          Stage 2: Risk Scoring
          6-Criteria Engine

          Evaluates Overdue Date, Pending ASN, Runway <2d, OTD <85%, Fill Rate <90%, High Criticality. Assigns 0-6 score.

          Node: Evaluate 6 Risk Criteria
          Stage 3: Decision Routing
          IF Conditional Filter

          Bypasses NORMAL POs to auto-pass logging. Routes FOLLOW_UP and ESCALATE to remediation.

          Nodes: Needs Supplier Email?, Update Sheet
          Stage 4: AI & Dispatch
          Gemini AI & Email

          Auto-drafts targeted escalation emails citing exact runway, SKU, and OTIF failure. Dispatches via Gmail with audit logging.

          Nodes: Gemini Chat Model, Log Execution

          The 6 Standardized Operational Risk Rules:

          1. Overdue Delivery: Expected_Date < Today
          2. Pending ASN: ASN_Status == 'Pending'
          3. Critical Runway: Inventory_Days < 2.0d
          4. Historical OTD Shortfall: Supplier_OTD < 85%
          5. Fill Rate Shortfall: Fill_Rate < 90%
          6. High Criticality: Material_Criticality == 'High'
          Workflow File: Export/PO Automation.json (19 nodes, n8n v2 schema)
